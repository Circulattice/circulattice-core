# Governance

The Circulattice community runs this research Lab. The LF Decentralized Trust Technical Advisory Council oversees the Labs program. Stewards handle proposal review and onboarding and can help refer disputes to the Council. Participation is open to individuals and organizations. The charter sets the intellectual-property terms and rules for community assets. [LFDT Labs charter](https://github.com/LF-Decentralized-Trust-labs/governance/blob/main/lf-decentralized-trust-labs_Charter.pdf) · [Labs governance](https://lf-decentralized-trust-labs.github.io/governance)

Use public issues to discuss design, workload assumptions and the project roadmap. Submit changes through reviewed pull requests. A change affecting financial semantics, checker invariants or benchmark methodology needs an issue or design proposal explaining the assumptions, alternatives and tests. Record the decision and any outstanding disagreements in the review so another contributor can follow it. Use the private reporting route for security-sensitive issues. [Approved scope and governance commitments](https://github.com/LF-Decentralized-Trust/lab-proposals/blob/main/proposals/circulattice.md) · [Security policy](SECURITY.md)

Repository maintainers review changes and record decisions in the relevant issue or pull request. Propose a change to the maintainer roster through the process in the maintainers file. Repository access is managed through the organization’s governance repository. [Maintainers](MAINTAINERS.md) · [Organization governance](https://github.com/Circulattice/governance)
